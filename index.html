TRASH
STASH™

The jobsite trash container that goes where you go.

Trash Stash™ keeps wire clippings, zip-tie ends, screws and small debris out of your pockets and off the floor. Clip it to your belt, pouch, ladder, cart or wall—then dump it cleanly when the job is done.

See How It WorksExplore Features
A better place for the little stuff

Small debris creates big frustration.

Trash Stash is a compact reusable container designed for tradespeople who need both hands free and a clean workspace. One-way top doors keep debris contained, while the bottom latch makes emptying fast and simple.

From belt to ladder to bin

How Trash Stash works

1

Clip it

Attach Trash Stash to your belt, tool pouch or modular mount.

2

Climb

Carry it hands-free while moving safely to the work area.

3

Use it

Drop clippings and debris through the one-way gravity doors.

4

Mount it

Move it from your belt to the ladder clip when convenient.

5

Finish

Complete the work and descend without loose debris in your pockets.

6

Quick dump

Open the lower latch and empty everything into a larger waste bin.
